# ReplaySpec pitch video storyboard

This video tells hackathon judges and developers that ReplaySpec turns a browser bug clip into evidence-backed regression proof.

| Scene | Beat / time | On screen | Story purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — The gap | Hook · 0–3.6s | ReplaySpec home hero and cart evidence preview. “A bug is easy to capture; proving the fix is the hard part.” | Opens on the unresolved question. |
| 02 — A traceable path | 3.6–9.5s | Recording → timestamped frames → evidence → regression check → Chromium verdict. | Shows the product’s central workflow. |
| 03 — Claims stay grounded | 9.5–16.6s | Real evidence inspector; OBSERVED, EXPECTED, HYPOTHESIS, and AMBIGUITY remain distinct. | Makes the evidence discipline visible. |
| 04 — AI assists, Chromium verifies | 16.6–22.9s | Optional paid Meta Llama 4 Maverick vision through OpenRouter; real recording workspace. | Keeps model assistance separate from the deterministic verdict. |
| 05 — The cart race | 22.9–29.6s | Matched 800 × 650 buggy and fixed screens: quantity 2 versus 3 after two clicks. | Supplies direct, uncropped proof of the defect and fix. |
| 06 — Three curated cases | 29.6–35.2s | Actual investigations list: checkout validation, cart race, and modal cancel. | Shows breadth through the working product. |
| 07 — Honest prototype scope | 35.2–40.8s | Bundled demo routes, local file-backed records, no real checkout; concise model data path. | States boundaries and provider disclosure clearly. |
| 08 — Close | 40.8–48.9s | ReplaySpec mark and “From frame to proof.” | Leaves the viewer with the product thesis. |

Rhythm: brief hook → measured workflow → evidence → AI boundary → cart proof → breadth → scope → quiet close. Runtime is approximately 49 seconds. Visuals use captured ReplaySpec screens and matched buggy/fixed demo states; all screens fit inside their frames. English captions are burned into the video and included as a separate SRT file. The voice is Microsoft George (offline, en-GB), slowed by 10% with pitch preserved; narration, scene timing, and subtitle cues share the same retime. No music bed, stock imagery, neon, or generic AI graphics.
